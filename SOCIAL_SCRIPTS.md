## Capture notes

- Record at 1080×1920 or crop a centered 9:16 region from desktop gameplay.
- Use a clean handle for the demo, and avoid showing private profile details.
- Let the nuclear shockwave breathe for a beat; it is the money shot.
- Keep the VO dry and deadpan. The game is already ridiculous.
- Add alt text to every post:

> A low-poly runner with a real X profile photo for a head runs through a sunset city, dodging barriers and unleashing a green nuclear fart shockwave.
